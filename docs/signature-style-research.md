# Signature Lab — style and movement

Research checked 22 September 2026 for The Spencerian Desk 1.3.0.

## What the references contribute

The six supplied images guide the composition: prominent open capitals, fine lowercase, selective dark shades, and broad curves around a readable name. The Kelchner signature sheet is especially useful for the balance between initials and surname. The pencil example shows how much of the character comes from shape alone. The ornate scrapbook and verse provide a richer display reference.

These are visual observations. A finished image does not establish writing time, pen lifts, or the original sequence of movements. The app's assembled signatures are new design concepts, not copies or authenticated historical signatures.

The constraints above are app design choices informed by instruction. The sources do not validate the app's generated signatures or supply performance data for its users. No web photographs or paid worksheets were copied into the app.

## Three starting compositions

1. **Rising finish:** the chosen full name with a prominent leading capital and a light upward exit.
2. **Open oval:** the chosen full name with a broad return through the space below the lettering.
3. **Initial signature:** an optional abbreviation of the first word, with the rest of the chosen name preserved and a sweeping return. The user must choose this changed signing text.

The original source letter outlines remain intact. Only the joins, layout, relative capital size, and added finish are composed. Source forms drawn at 50° are sheared by the angular difference to the 52° course target. Where an ending has no measured connection point, the renderer omits the added finish rather than inventing one.

## Practice and limits

Rehearse the finish lightly, then use it after the written name. Copy five examples and write five more from memory. Compare open spaces, clear letters, comfort, and consistency. Signing speed requires an actual writing trial; it cannot be inferred from the number of vector points. The app does not assign a speed or uniqueness score.

The finish study highlights the exact added curve. It does not animate outline contours as pen strokes or claim a verified historical stroke order.
